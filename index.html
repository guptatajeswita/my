Questions parents usually ask

What if my child is behind their grade level?

That's completely fine — the session starts from where your child actually is, not where the syllabus assumes they are. We adjust pace and examples live, based on how the group responds.

Is this a one-time session or an ongoing course?

This particular workshop on 30 November is a single 2-hour live session — a chance to experience our teaching approach directly, meet the instructor, and see if it's the right fit before committing to anything longer.

What materials does my child need?

Just a notebook, a pen, and a stable internet connection. A calculator isn't required — we intentionally focus on mental reasoning over tools.

Will there be individual attention in a group session?

Yes. Batch sizes are kept small specifically so every student gets called on, gets their working checked, and doesn't get lost in a crowd.
